    Sources & conventions

    Every factor is an exact definition from the sources below, or a named convention. Conversions go through the SI unit of each quantity as exact fractions (BigInt), so values like 0.1 never turn into 0.1000000000000000055. Rounding is half away from zero and happens only for display.

      • Mach uses the ICAO / ISA sea-level speed of sound, 340.294 m/s at 15 °C. Real Mach depends on air temperature.
      • Mercury and water columns (mmHg, inHg, inH₂O, mmH₂O) use the conventional densities in NIST SP 811: 13 595.1 kg/m³ (0 °C / 32 °F) and 1000 kg/m³, with g₀ = 9.806 65 m/s². Torr is exactly 101 325 / 760 Pa.
      • Survey vs international: foot, mile and acre default to the international foot (0.3048 m). US survey versions (1200/3937 m) are listed separately and are deprecated since 2023.
      • Calories & BTU: thermochemical (4.184 J) and International Table (4.1868 J) are separate units. BTU_IT is exactly 1055.055 852 62 J.
      • Horsepower: mechanical 550 ft·lbf/s, metric PS 75 kgf·m/s = 735.498 75 W, electric 746 W.
      • Data: SI prefixes (kB = 1000 B) and IEC binary prefixes (KiB = 1024 B) are kept apart.
      • Angles: degree, gon, arcminute, arcsecond, turn and NATO mil convert exactly. Radian units need π, which is carried to 75 decimal places.
      • Fuel economy: L/100 km and gal/100 mi are inverse units. 0 converts to ∞.
